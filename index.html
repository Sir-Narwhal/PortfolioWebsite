About

Engineering better processes through data.

I am a recent graduate (April 2024) of chemical engineering who is passionate about process control, optimization, and statistical modelling.

I am currently a graduate student at McMaster University studying a Master of Applied Science in the pursuit of developing soft sensors in biomanufacturing processes.

Outside of the lab I have many hobbies, including running, music, and programming. I actively participate in races, including 10 km and half-marathon events.

Research & Focus Areas

What I work on

Soft Sensors in Biomanufacturing

My MASc research at McMaster focuses on developing soft sensors — models that infer hard-to-measure process variables from readily available data — for biomanufacturing processes.

Current research

Process Control

Keeping chemical and biological processes stable, safe, and on target.

Optimization

Finding the best operating conditions to improve efficiency and performance.

Statistical Modelling

Turning process data into models that explain, predict, and inform decisions.
