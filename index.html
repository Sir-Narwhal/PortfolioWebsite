About

From plant floor to process model.

I’m currently an M.A.Sc. candidate in Chemical Engineering at McMaster University under Dr. Prashant Mhaskar. My thesis is on spectroscopy-driven concentration estimation for continuous monoclonal antibody manufacturing. Research & Focus Areas

What I work on

Spectroscopy-Driven Estimation for Continuous Antibody Manufacturing

Continuous biomanufacturing runs nonstop, so operators need to know protein concentrations as the process runs, not hours later from an offline sample. My M.A.Sc. thesis combines inline UV and Raman spectroscopy with multivariate modelling (PCA/PLS) and mechanistic state estimation to infer concentration in real time across a continuous monoclonal antibody process. That is the foundation for tighter monitoring, process analytical technology (PAT), and advanced control.

Current research · Dr. P. Mhaskar

Advanced Process Control

Model predictive control (MPC), PID tuning, and digital control design that keep processes stable, safe, and on target, even through disturbances.

State Estimation

Extended Kalman and particle filters that combine noisy measurements with process models to estimate states no sensor can see directly.

Process Modelling

Mechanistic ODE-based and dynamic models, calibrated to experimental data and written in state-space form so they can be used for estimation and control.

Multivariate Statistics

PCA and PLS for high-dimensional process and spectral data, turning thousands of correlated signals into models you can understand.

Optimization

LP, NLP, and MIP formulations in Pyomo and GAMS for equipment design, process economics, operations, and scheduling.

Industrial Automation

PLCs, SCADA, OPC-UA, and Modbus, plus the Industrial IoT and data infrastructure that connect equipment to people and algorithms.

In-House SCADA for Continuous Chromatography

I built an industrial SCADA system from the ground up to run the downstream train of a continuous monoclonal antibody process. It connects PLCs over OPC-UA and Modbus and uses Redis and MongoDB as a real-time data backbone, with automated alerts when the process drifts.

Data infrastructure

OPC-UA Server for Raw Spectroscopic Data

I engineered a Dockerized OPC-UA/UDP pipeline that expanded wavelength acquisition from 10 to 201 channels (about 20×). That made full-spectrum PCA/PLS modelling possible on what had been a heavily down-sampled signal.

Estimation & modelling

State Estimation of a Nonlinear Chromatography System

I calibrated a mechanistic, ODE-based chromatography model to experimental data and wrote it as a dynamic state-space model. Then I implemented custom Extended Kalman and particle filters to estimate bound and free protein concentrations in real time.
